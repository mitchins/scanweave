# Issue 37 review: do not approve

The candidate does remove the visible inverted text on the 10 reported pages. But it also rasterises whole pages that were rendering correctly, across most of the corpus. Its per-glyph local test misses most of the known damage, and it changes pages the issue never touched.

I didn't change anything in the checkout or the qualification folders. My scratch scripts are in `/tmp/ink37review`. All five candidate runs finished while I was reviewing, so the numbers below cover the full corpus.

## Confirmed results

**What works**
- **Segmentation is unchanged.** `segment()` isn't modified, and my Python copy of it reproduces main's paper and ink colours on all 32 pages.
- **Fallback pages are pixel-exact copies of the source.** On every fallback page in historical, financial, receipts and forms, the decoded image matches the input's own image. Main stored downsampled JPEG-compressed images instead.
- **The 10 reported pages lose all visible text:** historical 1–5 and receipts 4, 6–9.
- **mixed** is byte-identical to main.

**C1. Whole-page fallback fires on healthy pages.** Verified from the candidate's own reports and PDFs:

| Document | Pages that fall back | Visible glyphs lost | Size, main → candidate |
|---|---|---|---|
| financial | 1, 2, 5, 8, 10, 11, 13 (normal polarity) | 2,142 | 2.84 → 7.83 MB |
| forms | both (clean black on white) | 504 | — |
| receipts | 2 and 5 (not in the issue) | 19 | 1.27 → 10.26 MB |
| historical | all 5 (reported pages) | 58 | 2.25 → 34.5 MB |

- The cause is `all(...)` at `convert.rs:1316-1328`: one failing unit rasterises the whole page.
- The financial triggers are faint commas, apostrophes and specks (one or two per page). They're lighter than the page ink, so 0% of their pixels match it.
- The forms triggers are thin or small marks where fewer than 2% of the padded box's pixels match the ink colour.
- My copy of the tests predicted the fallback flag on all 32 pages before those runs finished, and every prediction was correct.

**C2. The local test misses most of the real #37 damage.** I applied `supported_local_ink` to main's damaged vector regions:
- It passes 10 of 22 on historical page 2 and 25 of 27 on page 5.
- The local brightness peak is dark (4–36), and 10–38% of pixels match the light "ink".
- So only the page test protects these pages.

**C3. The page test passes a page whose colours are inverted.** On `small_receipt` the corner "paper" is the dark table (brightness 35), the "ink" is the light receipt (230), and the dark table fills 78% of the interior. The test passes.
- The supplemental run proves nothing either way: neither run has any vector candidates (all regions are `ContactNonText`).
- **Conjecture:** dense or bold receipt text there would pass both tests, combining C2 and C3. That's the #37 failure. It needs a control that actually produces vector candidates.

**C4. Pages that didn't fall back still changed.** The donor filter at `convert.rs:410` changes reconstruction on financial pages 3 and 9, which lose 5 and 15 visible glyphs, and on page 6, whose image changed.
- Every financial page's content stream differs. That includes pages 4, 7 and 12, whose images are identical. I suspect glyph IDs were renumbered in the shared font, but haven't checked.
- Receipts pages 3 and 10 also didn't fall back; I didn't check whether they're byte-identical.

**C5. Copy text regressed in PDFium.** On financial page 1, two standalone "-" become U+FFFE and join the next word ("\ufffeJohn", "\ufffeTREASURER"). Poppler's physical-layout text gains one '¢' on historical page 5; its raw text is identical.

**C6. The fallback-reason counts are inflated.** `convert.rs:1332-1339` counts every vector region, including non-text regions with no units. Historical page 1 reports 295 `UncertainInkColour` regions although main had zero vector text there.

**C7. Code-level issues:**
- **Faithful profile not covered.** It's excluded (`convert.rs:1313`, `document.rs:375`) but still paints with the page-wide ink (`convert.rs:1985`). The faithful profile wasn't run, so whether it still shows the damage is unverified.
- **Repaired glyphs checked in the wrong place.** For repaired or reused glyphs the test checks `unit.bbox_px` (`:1325`), but the glyph is drawn at `replacement_bbox_px` using the donor's shape (`:1376-1383`).
- **Donors from fallback pages are still exported.** A page that falls back on local evidence still exports its other units as donors (`:378-411`). Combined with C2 and C3, donor safety isn't shown.
- **Reverse printing is never exercised.** `reverse_page` passes the page test but has no vector candidates in either run. The unit test feeds in hand-picked colour estimates, and `dark_border_does_not_support_inverted_estimates` has no border at all.
- **q3 glyph sharing is untested.** Every run used `--glyph-sharing off`.
- **Formatting:** the new `mask.rs` code adds rustfmt differences (HEAD was clean), and `:1331-1347` is mis-indented.

**C8. Several thresholds pass by thin margins.**
- `binding_shadow` matched-ink fraction is 0.0011 against a 0.001 threshold.
- receipts 5 falls back only on that fraction (0.0005).
- receipts 4's dominant brightness covers 0.11 of the interior against 0.10.
- In auto mode, historical's reconstruct and raster-baseline candidates are only 5.2 KB apart.

## Required before re-review

1. **Demote per region, not per page, on local failure.** Keep whole-page fallback only for the page-level polarity check. Run the local check only on glyphs that are actually drawn, at their placement box with the shape actually drawn.
2. **Replace or back up the local test with one that doesn't depend on which side is bright**, such as the foreground share in a padded OCR box. Validate it against the 65 known-bad objects (target 0 passes) and every glyph on financial and forms (target 0 false failures).
3. **Make donor eligibility use the same rule as the page's final decision.** Then measure the effect on financial pages 3, 6 and 9.
4. **Add controls that actually produce vector candidates:**
   - a small receipt with bold text on a dark table;
   - true reverse printing with visible vectors whose fill is checked against the glyph pixels;
   - faint punctuation and hairline glyphs on clean pages.
5. **Corpus runs in faithful and ClearScan, with q3 sharing on and off, and in auto mode.** Every unaffected page should be byte-identical, or every difference explained.
6. **PDFium and Poppler text identity for every page,** including a fix for the U+FFFE hyphen.
7. **Correct the fallback counts** to exclude non-text regions, add a document-level flag, and add convert-level tests that cover the report fields.
