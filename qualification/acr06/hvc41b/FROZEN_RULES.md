# HVC-41B frozen research rules

Frozen before HVC-41B pilot or control outputs are examined. These are conservative experimental screening rules, not production policy.

* Region polarity: use only source RGB within the region's OCR symbol boxes. Relative to each box's 85th-percentile luminance, dark pixels are at least 30 luminance units darker; require 5–45% dark pixels, at least 60 luminance units between 15th and 85th percentiles, at least 70% of the box perimeter within 24 luminance units of its 85th percentile, and that percentile within 24 luminance units of the independently estimated interior paper. Require each box to contain at least six dark pixels. Regions with any failing box are raster-only. Ink fill is the per-channel median of the dark pixels in the admitted region's boxes intersected with its owned source mask, excluding artwork and border components.
* Page-border component: its bounding box touches at least two distinct page edges and covers at least 80% of both page width and height. Only those components receive a Euclidean four-source-pixel proximity refinement after the existing bbox filter. All other artwork and all suspect/protected checks stay unchanged.
* Per-glyph candidate validation, after rendering the completed PDF at source geometry: source reference is the local dark-relative mask above, within the glyph OCR box. Require vector-mask IoU >= 0.90, absolute vector-fill vs source-ink median luminance error <= 15/255, and zero source-ink pixels removed outside the glyph-owned mask. Require no newly painted vector pixels outside the glyph box expanded by one source pixel. Reject individually on any missing metric or failure. Do not count OCR labels as character verification. Review accepted glyphs visually against the source; retained background source pixels must remain intact outside accepted ownership.
* Freeze these thresholds; no retuning after pilot results. No page-ID admission. Controls can remain fully raster.
