# Proposed detector-review queue

Use existing Phase 1b aligned PDFium source-DPI raw scores and flags, not a revised detector or threshold. Within each page × row × detector, rank flagged instances by the empirical percentile of their raw score. Sort descending percentile, then descending raw score, row, box and detector for deterministic ties. Percentiles allow a ranking across detectors with different score units; raw scores remain in the index and the full source scores remain local. This is a workload rule, not a calibrated probability of a defect.

Select at most 10 flagged crops per page and at most three per pixel-distinct output row. Greedily skip boxes that overlap a selected box at IoU ≥ 0.5; compare exact RGB crop bytes across rows and skip source-identical candidates, recording equivalent output rows once. The rule deduplicates overlapping boxes even when another output differs, which can miss row-specific damage. Add two fixed-seed random non-overlapping controls per page, drawn from the existing per-page × row controls. This gives a maximum of 12 crops/page and a nominal 20 seconds/crop. Controls are not assumed defect-free; no detector accuracy is inferred from them.

Coverage counts the original flagged candidate instances, including duplicate detectors and pixel-identical rows, whose box overlaps a selected candidate at IoU ≥ 0.5 and whose output pixels equal its selected row over the selected crop. This denominator is not the number of unique defects. The four reviewed panel locations are checked for both overlap and concerned-row pixel equivalence. All prior AI assessments are provisional, and these prior human-panel selections are not an independent detector evaluation. Local crops are for planning; no new human-panel records are created or modified.
