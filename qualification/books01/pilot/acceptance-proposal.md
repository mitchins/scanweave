# Revised acceptance proposal — not frozen

This is a proposal for owner review, not a Phase 2 rule or a ranking. All existing crop verdicts cited below are **AI-assessed, provisional (not human-adjudicated)**. A detector suspicion or an AI assessment triggers review; only a human-confirmed visual defect can disqualify an output on defect grounds. Unreviewed, ambiguous, unsupported-registration or insufficient-GT cases are **pending**, not passes. Automated metrics are screening evidence and may prompt review; they cannot by themselves establish a visible wrong glyph.

## Proposed criteria

1. **Visible content.** Compare the original and globally aligned renders, preserving native metrics and geometry separately. Screen for text-region SSIM ≥ 0.98 and ink recall ≥ 0.99, paper SSIM ≥ 0.98, and, where illustration polygons exist, illustration SSIM ≥ 0.98 and mean ΔE2000 ≤ 3. A failed or undefined screen requires human review. Reject for a human-confirmed wrong-character shape, missing significant text/ink, materially damaged illustration or other material loss of page content. Review punctuation, diacritics and fine strokes individually; there is no minimum defect size.
2. **OCR and search usability.** On the *same matched GT lines*, propose PDF CER no more than 0.05 absolute and WER no more than 0.10 absolute above frozen hOCR, with at least five shared lines and 100 known GT characters. Matcher-unmatched GT character share should be no more than the frozen-hOCR share plus 0.10; unmatched is a matcher result and requires inspection before calling a line missing. Require at least 90% PDFium search hits among five or more sampled words correctly recognised by frozen OCR, and at least 50% hits among the sampled GT terms; report control false hits and order τ. Below-coverage samples are pending. This is a **relative** requirement: poor recognition in the matched baseline remains an explicit limitation even if visual preservation and relative extraction pass.
3. **Geometry and cropping.** A single global transform must meet the predeclared registration rule. Propose allowing straightening up to 1° and uniform scale 0.995–1.005, subject to independent review of page boundaries and any lost GT text, artwork or other meaningful content. Any source-frame area outside the mapped output, changed page box, larger transform, or unsupported registration requires review; reject only if loss or unacceptable geometry is human-confirmed. Never apply local warps or hide geometry change in an aligned fidelity score.
4. **Evidence and size.** Human-confirmed defects and AI/detector suspicions are separate. Suspicions and screen failures create review items, never automatic glyph-defect confirmations. Only after visual, OCR/search and geometry requirements have been resolved should byte size be compared among eligible outputs. `sw-build` is diagnostic and is never ranked.

## Worked pilot examples

- **Illustrative conditional acceptance:** on French page 12342014, OCRmyPDF's globally aligned text SSIM and ink recall are both 1.000, its transform is identity, and the same 70 matched GT lines give frozen-hOCR CER 0.477 versus PDF CER 0.484 and WER 0.786 versus 0.789. It hits 11/11 sampled words recognised by frozen OCR and 15/20 GT search terms. It would meet these *numeric* screens, subject to human visual and boundary review. The frozen OCR's known-character bag recall is only 0.645: the page can be visually faithful while its OCR remains seriously limited.
- **Illustrative conditional rejection:** on Luther 0011, the MRC output is much smaller, but crop 0018 contains provisionally assessed missing and malformed strokes. If a human confirms the material loss, it would be rejected regardless of size or searchability. Adobe on page 8967333 has similarly provisional broken-glyph findings in crops 0055 and 0056; rejection requires human confirmation. Adobe here means **Acrobat default OCR language (not matched); no Fraktur support; page straightened (deskew)**.
- **Pending, not accepted:** page 8967333 has just three single-line GT proxies covering 32 known characters. It cannot satisfy the proposed line-coverage floor, so passing region-level image metrics or search alone is insufficient to declare it OCR-eligible.

These floors are initial screening proposals: the source-preserving OCRmyPDF pilot control was at 1.000 for PDFium source-DPI text SSIM and ink recall. Six pages, small provisional reference sets, matcher limitations and a nonblinded fresh-control assessment do not validate perceptual thresholds. Owner review and the blinded human panel are required before freezing the rule.
