# Completed coherent-residual C: independent visual gate

**Disposition: visually acceptable on the inspected primary pages and controls, with a disclosed raster-softness tradeoff.** This is approval to qualify an integrated implementation, not proof of arbitrary-document safety or a review of production changes.

Actual `qualification/acr05/local/C.pdf` was rendered against source, ACR04, Adobe and the previously blinded Y. Primary financial page indices 0,1,10,12 and corrected control indices **6,7** were inspected. Earlier baseline index5 is an additional control, not a substitute for index7. Both-engine fixed-coordinate detail sheets and normal-view pairs are retained under `local/c-review`; `render-review.py` preserves the detailed comparison. No implementation code or additional source erasure was used in this visual judgement.

## Findings

- The X-style rectangular borders around digits, dust specks and rule fragments are absent from C in the inspected windows. Paper is coherent, including the specific supplied worst native-patch boxes.
- Source-supported amounts, counters and punctuation remain human-readable: selected examples include `15 00`, `4 00`, `$295 00`, `185`, `5`, `1`, and the control rows `32 75`, `33 75`, `22 00`. The table's separated dots/ellipsis remain visible; source dollar symbols and thin subtotal rules survive.
- **Unsupported raster text is visibly softer at 300 DPI.** This is clear in page0 SELECTMEN/Moulton and retained portions of body words, compared with ACR04 and Y. The change should not be described as purely invisible packaging or an across-the-board clarity improvement. In these selected windows the softer text remains readable and does not become a different character. Normal-size complete pages remain coherent.
- Font-shaped text retains inherited outline traits and can appear sharper than adjacent unsupported raster words. This is a representation/composition distinction, not evidence that C improved the font contours. Existing imperfect glyphs remain existing imperfections.
- No new gross halo, duplicated full-strength text, missing rule, rectangular hole, or source-mark deletion was identified in the inspected full pages/details in either renderer. Minor texture/edge-tone changes are permitted by the stated ClearScan objective; faithful is a separate preservation path.
- Normal-view pairs on all six requested pages show no obvious new page-geometry or decoding defect. This is a small representative review, not every-mark certification of the thirteen-page collection.

The preferred candidate is C for coherent paper without X's local boxes; Y was already preferable to X, but C trades some native raster sharpness for its consistent residual representation. Its smaller reported bytes are not the visual acceptance argument. Require exact existing glyph/Unicode/ownership checks and the actual integrated output before product promotion; do not generalize from the shadow derivative alone.
