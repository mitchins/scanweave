# Genuine development pages

The ten receipts are the first ten **training** rows of [CORD v2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/naver-clova-ix/cord-v2), by the CORD authors at Clova AI. The [project source and licence](https://github.com/clovaai/cord) specify **CC BY 4.0**; the downloaded licence is `cord_license.txt`. Original receipt JPEGs, RGB PNG conversions, OCR, ClearScan PDFs and render comparisons retain this attribution. Changes: decode to PNG, text-region cropping for OCR, reconstructed vectors, page-background composition and PDF rendering. Original full-page imagery remains the composition input. Annotation text was not supplied to Tesseract or ScanWeave; annotation boxes only select the receipt text region.

The five historical pages are from [OCR-D/gt_structure_text](https://github.com/OCR-D/gt_structure_text), revision `379f597a8dada882e6c2e4f509dbf9fdf2ebffa1`. The dataset authors and contributing libraries are credited by that repository and the exact original paths in `downloads.json`. Data are **CC BY-SA 4.0**: see `ocrd-LICENSE.md`. The corresponding PNGs, PDFs and rendered derivative comparisons are distributed under the same licence. Changes: TIFF-to-RGB-PNG conversion, existing English Tesseract OCR and ScanWeave reconstruction/composition. No source-font oracle exists for these pages.

`downloads.json` records exact original paths, hashes and sizes. Receipt signed asset URLs expire; `fetch.py` resolves the stable public training endpoint again and verifies frozen hashes. Historical original TIFFs may be fetched rather than duplicated in Git. `region-ocr-manifest.json` records crop geometry and exact commands. Physical scan DPI is not established for these public photographs/images; 300 DPI is the converter's **nominal placement geometry**, not a claim about capture resolution.
