# ACR-03 independent visual Gate B review

## Scope

Read-only inspection of actual ACR-03 comparison outputs for the five fixed cases:
logo, crossing annotation, red seal, financial text/totals and shaded book page.
Both PDFium and Poppler region sheets and full-page overviews were examined.
Additional local crops showed the candidate columns at their sheet resolution,
without resizing. Source, frozen Adobe, ACR-01, rejected ACR-02, candidate A
(native object crops), candidate B (masked native-colour tiles), and evaluator-only
oracle objects where present were distinguished.

Minor source-pixel/tonal differences are permitted. Judgment concerns material
readability, document colour and clean composition, not pixel-exact preservation.
No production edits, new experiment or holdout access was performed. This is a
selected-case visual gate, not full-product qualification or a compression gate.

## Candidate A — object crops

**Passes the selected-case visual Gate B.**

- The blue logo has a clean uniform fill without ACR-02's nested triangular bands.
  The S and W are blue, restoring the source appearance; the inherited ACR-01
  dark S is no longer painted as a conflicting visible font.
- The crossing blue annotation and horizontal/vertical rules remain clear. Payment
  amount, reference/account identifiers and punctuation remain readable. No new
  duplicate text or erased intersection was identified.
- The seal remains recognizably red, including its wording and circular boundary.
  This avoids ACR-02's material colour loss. Nearby reverse print and stains remain
  visible in the inspected view.
- Financial values 185, 5, 1, 7, 4, 1, 1, total 204 and its adjacent punctuation,
  and the underline survive. ACR-02's conspicuous circular cleanup halos at the
  rule/total are absent in the inspected A view.
- The shaded book edge retains green colour and adjacent paper remains visually
  continuous in the inspected A crop. No obvious new rectangle seam or double
  edge was identified.

This supports A's source-object preservation mechanism on the supplied cases. It
is not a claim that the canonical dictionary improved or that all source marks
are proven safe. Historical output growth must remain a separate byte-selection
failure/fallback decision. The reported financial single-page intervention is not
full thirteen-page collection qualification.

## Candidate B — masked native-colour tiles

**Does not pass Gate B across the complete fixed case set.** Logo, annotation,
seal colour and financial characters remain readable in inspected regions, and
B avoids ACR-02's logo contour bands and red-seal colour loss. However, the shaded
book corner develops an artificial broad smoothed tan region beneath the book
edge. Fine paper appearance changes abruptly against the retained surrounding
samples, with visible transition/edge structure and coloured flecks near the
boundary. This appears in both engines; it is a compositing/representation seam,
not merely a demand to preserve every paper-grain pixel. The full-page view is
less conspicuous than the fixed crop, but does not negate the local defect.

No specific newly wrong numeral or identifier was established for either A or B.
That limited readability finding does not override B's failed composition case.

## Conclusion

A is visually credible for the next already-authorized matched qualification step,
subject to actual complete-PDF savings and remaining semantic/mechanical gates.
B must not be treated as generally qualified from these results. No further
candidate, threshold change or representation method is requested by this review.
Actual source/native ownership, hidden-font Unicode and dictionary invariants
require their separate code and machine-readable evidence. The oracle column is
an evaluator-only reference, not evidence that inferred object detection is perfect.

## Eligibility-scoped clarification

The coordinator subsequently proposed explicit fallback for pages with unknown
paper outside retained coherent objects, rather than tuning B to pass the shaded
page. On the already-inspected **logo, annotation and financial** cases alone,
B is visually acceptable under this contract: clean logo fill/blue lettering,
readable crossing text/rules, and preserved financial digits/punctuation without
the prior circular halos. No material new seam was identified in those cases.
This is an eligible-subset pass, not withdrawal of the shaded-page failure.

The unknown-paper audit and actual fallback implementation need independent code
and mechanical verification; this visual review does not establish their
correctness from the images. Historical shade must remain fallback, and no
historical-seal B qualification is implied if its model/byte gates fail. Full
financial-document size/semantics qualification remains pending. No general A or B
promotion is recommended solely from these selected views.
