# M1-D document-wide reconstruction qualification

## Result

The cross-page repair path works for several damaged glyphs, but document-wide
font reuse and compression do not pass qualification. Keep M1-D open and do
not promote the ClearScan reconstruction candidate. The strict per-occurrence
0.98 source-mask preservation gate rejects all 33 proposed intact-glyph reuse
candidates at each resolution. In `auto` mode the smaller searchable raster
baseline is selected, so the unsafe, larger reconstruction is not emitted.

The fixture contains 12 synthetic pages at each of 300 and 600 DPI, using
DejaVu Serif and Liberation Serif, primary and alternate styles, damaged `e`,
`a`, and `8` examples, a protected amount, and a visual `e` labeled as `c`.
Images and evidence are generated by
`qualification/experiments/m1d_document_fixture.py`; the outline font is
known to the evaluator and is not embedded as test ground truth in the PDF.

## Measured outcome

| DPI | Repaired | Pristine IoU before → final | Reuse accepted | Vector ink coverage | ClearScan PDF | Raster baseline | Size change |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 300 | `e`, `a` | `e` 0.831 → 0.918; `a` 0.836 → 0.931 | 0 | 2.23% | 104,188 B | 72,591 B | 31,597 B larger |
| 600 | `e`, `a`, `8` | `e` 0.847 → 0.958; `a` 0.829 → 0.967; `8` 0.913 → 0.918 | 0 | 3.98% | 173,180 B | 143,927 B | 29,253 B larger |

The 300-DPI `8` is rejected as an unsupported repair. Its rendered ClearScan
region scores 0.732 pristine IoU against 0.912 for the source observation,
despite the fallback image XObject containing the exact source crop. This is
an unresolved local-patch rendering/placement discrepancy and is an additional
visual-safety failure. The 600-DPI `8` passes the independent truth comparison,
but improves only 0.005 IoU over the source.

PDFium provides a second visual warning. At nominal size, pristine IoU for
ClearScan `e`/`a`/`8` is 0.784/0.803/0.768 at 300 DPI and
0.882/0.884/0.907 at 600 DPI. At 4x zoom those scores are
0.881/0.893/0.862 and 0.938/0.929/0.937, respectively. Search works, but
appearance does not yet agree robustly across renderers.

The fixture reports zero false substitutions and zero mixed-style prototype
groups. Protected financial characters remain raster-owned, and the visual
`e` mislabeled as `c` is not repaired. Searchable text is identical between
faithful and ClearScan PDFs, and PDFium finds the target glyphs on every page.

PDFium independent rendering at 4x zoom is present as
`dpi300/pdfium-clearscan-zoom4x.png` and
`dpi600/pdfium-clearscan-zoom4x.png`; nominal and zoomed per-glyph metrics are
in each `evaluation.json`. The final font passes fontTools validation and OTS
sanitization. `qpdf --check` passes on ClearScan, faithful, truth, and raster
baseline PDFs.

## Limits and next engineering issue

The evaluator uses the original input images, a same-renderer raster PDF
baseline, faithful mode, ClearScan mode, and pristine synthetic PDF renders.
It does not include a separately frozen pre-M1-D binary or an OCRmyPDF/MRC
baseline. The current forced reconstruction PDFs are larger than the raster
baseline and are not a compression benefit. The regression harness also does
not qualify a production receipt/book corpus.

Independent review agrees that the donor-supported `e` and `a` repairs are
valid, the 600-DPI `8` is only marginally beneficial, and the document-wide
reuse/compression objective fails. Keep the 0.98 reuse gate. The next required
work is to fix traced-glyph placement/shape fidelity and local raster-patch
rendering, then repeat the matched-renderer qualification before enabling
cross-page reuse.
