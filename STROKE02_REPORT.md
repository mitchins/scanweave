# STROKE-02 — corrected crop experiment

**The old forward-consistency rejection was invalid.** Correcting subpixel registration and comparing each PDF at its own best effective blur materially improves the experimental crop scores. A layered crop beats MRC's measured error on all three 613 boxes and both French regions, but the best-scoring 6 has no detected counter; the tested counter-preserving solid shape scores slightly worse than MRC. There is no improved ScanWeave PDF, and the whole-page and integration prerequisites are not yet established.

Actual PDFium panels: [613 at 1×](/data/coding/scanweave/work/books01/stroke02/final/613-1x.png), [613 at 2×](/data/coding/scanweave/work/books01/stroke02/final/613.png), [French at 1×](/data/coding/scanweave/work/books01/stroke02/final/french-1x.png), [French at 2×](/data/coding/scanweave/work/books01/stroke02/final/french.png). The labelled experimental row is a **crop PDF over source raster, without OCR**, not an improved ScanWeave output. Competitor PDFs use the existing registration.

1. **613 versus MRC.** The layered crop is smoother and more legible than #39 and visually competitive at 1×/2×, but its 6's counter is not detected in the rendered-coverage topology check. The 6's counter-preserving alternative has one component and one hole for σ=.3–.93, yet its best measured error is .1106 versus MRC's .1030. Raising the isocontour lowers the error to .0767, but loses the detected hole. A further layered candidate forced the source-supported counter pixels clear before tracing; it scored .0552–.0662 but still had no detected hole after PDF rendering. These are image-derived coverage thresholds and clipped OCR-group boxes, not independent glyph ground truth. There are no detected cross-group joins in the numeral candidates.
2. **French.** The experimental crop looks clearer and smoother than #39; the local-ink contours are darker and it is not established that fine serifs, punctuation and counters are preserved. Across the tested σ values, the two French line regions change component and hole counts. Components crossing groups stay raster. Therefore a lower image error alone does not establish the requested safe improvement.
3. **Changes and corrections.** The fourfold contour writer had a half-pixel offset and an inconsistent interpolation grid. It now uses grid-aligned interpolation and PDF pixel-edge coordinates. A known rectangle survives PDFium rendering with centroid error below .1 source pixel and mean coverage error below .02. The earlier `crop_fit.py` path already added the pixel-center correction. The former `RMSE ≤ paper MAD` decision has been removed. A known-latent synthetic PDF with assumed Gaussian noise matching each local residual proxy and JPEG quality 95 has 95th-percentile oracle residuals .0085–.0123 for the first two digit regions, exceeding the .0072/.0078 proxies. This is a calibration under **assumed** compression/noise, not a measured distribution for the scans.

   The prior heading-derived σ interval must not be applied to the numerals. In the current local, straight-box cross-section model, the minimum profile residuals occur at σ=.7/.93/.7 for the three digit boxes and .7/.7 for the French regions. These are grid minima, **not calibrated confidence intervals**: the model has nontrivial residual and curved profiles are not perfect boxes. Each output below is instead compared with the source after independently minimizing a Gaussian effective reblur over [0, 1.5] px in .1 px steps. Registered PDFium renders are compared in linear luminance inside the same boxes; raster pixels retained in the crop participate. This measures resemblance after reblur, not character correctness or compression.

4. **PDF size and text coverage.** Neither was improved or measured in a whole-page candidate. Existing baseline bytes for 613 source/#39/MRC/Adobe are 1,691,991 / 92,310 / 105,242 / 151,635; for French they are 4,955,345 / 554,944 / 137,040 / 396,265. The crop PDFs retain raster and cannot prove displacement, OCR/search preservation or speed.
5. **Remaining regressions and evidence gaps.** The counter-versus-fit tradeoff remains; French topology is not stable across the exploratory grid. The σ grids are sensitivity checks, not fitted confidence intervals. Aventinus, Luther, Euler and the coloured heading have not been rerun with a product candidate; the pre-existing U+FFFE issue remains unresolved. Improving the counter-preserving tonal representation and calibrating local ink/background remain testable; neither an impossible-reconstruction claim nor a within-noise veto is justified.
