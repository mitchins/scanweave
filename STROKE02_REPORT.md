# STROKE-02 — guarded component extensions, 36-page result

**The converter experiment does not meet the restoration objective and is not ready to merge.** All 36 development PDFs completed. The default-off path admitted some local masks, but it did not improve 613, show a convincing French-list gain, or reduce the overall raster burden. Source, #39, experimental ScanWeave, MRC and Adobe strips come from actual PDFs; no MRC or Adobe conversion was rerun.

1. **613 versus MRC:** No. The actual candidate is byte-identical to #39 and the numerals remain fragmented and shell-coloured. The per-line fit for the label `[1174,756,1225,785]` hit its bounds (σ=1.50 px, t=1.50, tmax=1.50; proposed RGB 111,106,44), so the pre-pass declined it instead of applying that unreliable brown estimate. The three original digit units contain 34, 10 and 23 foreground pixels and remain visible; none receives an extension. Their reconstruction records say `no_supported_donor` with source-mask IoU 1.0. In the actual PDF counter check, the candidate equals #39: one source-near-paper pixel exceeds twice the local residual-noise proxy (0.0072 linear luminance); this is an existing defect, and the proxy is not a calibrated noise estimate. Earlier source core medians are warm (155,152,128), (169.5,168,135.5), (166,164,132): warm ink is supported; the declined extrapolation is not a reliable colour measurement.
2. **French print:** The visual strip does not establish clearer text. Thirteen proposals (326 pixels) passed extension checks, but reported vector ink fell from 150,562 to 148,147 pixels and raster bytes rose by 633. Of 441 attempted units, 281 crossed the unit box and 147 hit the conservative enclosed-paper margin. For example `w000072s0006` grows from 169 to 207 mask pixels but becomes invisible; its unchanged reconstruction gate records source-mask IoU 0.716 and `rejected_unchanged_source_fidelity`. The unchanged `UnsupportedContent` region decision moved six regions from vector to raster and two in the opposite direction. This is evidence of the next limiting stage, not a reason to relax it.
3. **What changed:** An offline per-line pre-pass estimates local paper, ink and blur/coverage from OCR geometry and observed pixels; OCR text does not prescribe shapes. `SCANWEAVE_STROKE_COVERAGE` enables an experimental per-unit path, leaving page-wide segmentation, paper and polarity unchanged. New pixels must stay inside the original unit box, connect to an original component owned by that unit, and remain at least two pixels from other components; a component-ID-to-pixels ledger tracks them. An explicit paper veto declines the entire unit within a conservative three-pixel contour/PSF margin, and ambiguous fits decline. A strengthened flag-only invariant checks all owned-mask pixels and rejects artwork overlap. Existing contact, IoU, ownership and other gates are unchanged. Per-symbol fill colour is passed through the PDF-import page assembly only for accepted, vector-owned symbols; stock colour is the fallback. This is a research pre-pass and default-off converter path, not a production integration.

5. **Regressions and safeguards:** All four control PDFs and 613 are byte-identical to #39, so the inspected 1×/2× crops cannot regress relative to it. Nine other PDFs changed; their overall visual safety is not established. PDFium text changes occur on 9684354 (one whitespace insertion), 12342014 (two whitespace replacements) and 12342041 (three whitespace replacements). No non-whitespace character or order changes were detected. Ownership changed for 179 visible→invisible and 151 invisible→visible occurrences; this may affect selection despite equivalent character sequences. The edit-position audit found no new U+FFFE; 113 pre-existing occurrences remain in both extractions, and are not acceptable as a new behavior. [Extraction audit](research/stroke02/extraction-audit.json). Full `scanweave-io` tests passed; flag-off 613 and a French run with the veto file missing each reproduced #39 byte-for-byte.
6. **Ready to merge?** No. The local fit needs reliable per-region calibration, the paper veto is deliberately over-conservative, and the unchanged region/trace gates can reject or rasterise extended masks. There is no demonstrated overall compression or legibility gain. Do not promote the flag.

**Safeguard gap in #39:** The default ownership invariant validates assigned component IDs, not newly added mask pixels. A constructed artwork-overlap case passes that original invariant but fails the new flag-only pixel invariant. In practice #39's `visual_dict::refine_observed_mask` adds pixels outside the original page foreground to cloned Otsu-refined prototypes: the French-page audit found 24 of 66 recorded prototype entries, with 194 added-pixel occurrences (repeated witnesses possible). These are prototype clones, not observed mutations of the original owned-unit masks. The default invariant remains unchanged. [Audit](research/stroke02/baseline-mask-audit.json).
