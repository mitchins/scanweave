# STROKE-02 — joint local-ink and counter test

**The product objective remains unmet.** The new per-digit candidate is not demonstrably as readable and natural as MRC at 1× and 2×; its 613 strokes look too dark and its French outlines too heavy. There is no improved ScanWeave PDF. The earlier five-hypothesis agreement veto has been removed; these results test the owner's forward-consistency and topology criteria instead. No converter code or safety gate changed.

Actual PDFium comparisons: [613, 2×](/data/coding/scanweave/work/books01/stroke02/final/613.png), [613, 1×](/data/coding/scanweave/work/books01/stroke02/final/613-1x.png), [French, 2×](/data/coding/scanweave/work/books01/stroke02/final/french.png), [French, 1×](/data/coding/scanweave/work/books01/stroke02/final/french-1x.png). Rows are source, #39, unavailable improved ScanWeave, a separately labelled experimental crop PDF, MRC and Adobe. The crop PDF contains vector paths over source raster and **no OCR**; it is not ScanWeave output or evidence of compression. Existing competitor render registrations are applied.

1. **613 versus MRC.** Not yet competitive. Five σ values spanning the measured .93–1.48 px IQR were tested per digit. Two distinct contour representations were tried at each σ, plus five counter-preserving variants for the 6. The consistent set is **empty for each digit**, independently, using reblurred actual 2× PDF renders against the source and the local noise proxy. No observed cross-group join was introduced. Solid 1 has stable (component, hole) = (1, 0); the solid 6 and 3 each have (2, 0), so these diagnostic counts do not establish correct character identity. The counter variant of the 6 alternates between (1, 1) and (2, 0) across σ. The counts depend on a coverage threshold and OCR boxes can clip strokes.

   | Group | Best rendered-rescan luma RMSE | Local paper residual MAD | Profile RMSE, best joint fit | Consistent set |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | 6 | .1322 | .0072 | .0490 | 0 |
   | 1 | .1212 | .0078 | .0386 | 0 |
   | 3 | .1100 | .0132 | .0395 | 0 |

   Values are linear luminance. The MAD is a **noise/model-error proxy**, not independently calibrated sensor noise. The PDF rescan test reblurs retained raster near contour boundaries too, so its residual is conservative and can overstate a vector candidate's error. The profile test independently fits isolated cross-sections and still exceeds the same proxy; free amplitude per profile leaves minima .0473/.0304/.0376. These tests show the proposed candidates and simple box model do not explain the observations under the tested assumptions; they do not rule out a more complete imaging model.

2. **French text.** Better clarity without bolding is not demonstrated. For two selected text regions, per-line profile fits choose t=1.00 and σ=.80/.65 px (the page's step-edge median is .93 px). Ten rendered variants per region have minimum rescan RMSE .0778/.0616 versus local residual MAD .0213/.0176, and topology varies across the tested σ values. Some source components intersect grouping boxes and remain raster. Punctuation and historic controls were not changed or independently validated with a product candidate.
3. **What changed mathematically?** The ink direction comes from each digit or line's own core observations, with `K=P+t(C-P)` and nonnegative RGB limiting t; no heading colour participates. Isolated cross-sections share t while center and width vary; their box profiles include Gaussian blur and pixel integration. For every σ the crop prototype deconvolves fractional coverage, traces bicubic 4× subpixel contours, fits cubic curves and renders solid or fractional-opacity paths. A candidate must fit within the local residual proxy, preserve topology over σ and avoid unsupported cross-group joins; among passing candidates it chooses minimum rendered ink mass, then width regularity. None passed. OCR supplies grouping only, not shapes. An isolated synthetic four-pixel bar recovers width 4.04 px and amplitude within .01, but this sanity test does not validate curved, faded text.

   **Counter (C3):** within the 15 source pixels proposed as the 6's counter, a counter beats a filled version by .138–.247 linear-luma RMSE across the σ/t grid, holding surrounding latent coverage fixed. Both alternatives still have substantial residual (best .106 versus .307), and the proposed region and surrounding field are model-dependent. The evidence favors a counter conditional on that model; the counter-preserving actual PDF remains topology-unstable and fails forward consistency. The 6 stays raster; 1 and 3 also fail their own tests, not a veto based on the 6.
4. **PDF size and text coverage.** No candidate full-page PDF exists; no raster displacement, size benefit, OCR/search preservation or candidate render time has been established. Existing comparator sizes are:

5. **Remaining problems.** C1 fails for the tested PDF candidates, with model/noise limitations as above. C2's physically bounded joint fit does not explain the measured profiles to within the proxy; unknown fading, local paper/ink variation, curved strokes or an inadequate PSF/profile model remain plausible causes. C3 favors the counter but does not yet produce a safe stable reconstruction. French remains too heavy. Aventinus, Luther, Euler and the coloured heading were not rerun: the crop prerequisite for whole-page conversion was not satisfied. The baseline U+FFFE bug remains unresolved; no new extraction was produced.
6. **Ready to merge?** No. No 36-page or converter integration run was justified by the crop results. The next technical work would require validating local background/ink and a spatially varying scan model against independently measured noise and better-isolated curved-stroke observations, then rechecking actual rendered topology and legibility. This is a limitation of the tested model, not proof that reconstruction is impossible.
